# NCAA DIII men's soccer tournament bracket, edited by hand. The site's Bracket tab
# shows it as soon as there's at least one game under a round; until then it shows
# the "announced" date and the latest NPI as teams to watch.
#
# When the bracket comes out (ncaa.com/championships/soccer-men/d3):
# 1. Add a "## <round>" line, then one line per game: team | team | score | note
#    - "### <group>" lines (a host site or pod) are optional, under a round
#    - leave score empty until it's played: Williams | Amherst | | Sat. 11 a.m.
#    - score is first team first; ties add PKs: Tufts | Bowdoin | 1-1 (4-3 PK) |
#    - a first-round bye: Hamilton | Bye
#    - team names can be short ("CWRU", "Wash. & Lee"), like the NPI table
# 2. Update the "as of" line and commit. The site redeploys itself.
#
# Example:
#   ## First round
#   ### Williamstown, Mass.
#   Williams | Bye
#   Amherst | Vassar | 2-1 OT | Sat., Nov. 14
#
